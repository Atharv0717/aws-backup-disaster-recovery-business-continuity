🚀 Just completed a hands-on AWS project: **Backup, Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity (Mumbai ➜ Singapore)**

What happens to your business when an EC2 instance, an EBS volume, a database or even an entire AWS Region goes down? I built and tested the answer. 👇

🔹 Deployed Nginx on EC2 across 2 Availability Zones
🔹 Attached EBS and shared an encrypted EFS file system between instances
🔹 S3 versioning + Cross-Region Replication + lifecycle rules
🔹 RDS MySQL in private subnets
🔹 Automated EBS snapshots (Data Lifecycle Manager) + AMIs copied to Singapore
🔹 AWS Backup: daily plan, 7-day retention, vaults and cross-Region copy
🔹 Simulated failures and RECOVERED: EC2 from AMI, EBS volume from backup, RDS from snapshot in a different Region ✅

💡 Key learnings
• Backups are useless until you test the restore
• Cross-Region copies = real protection against Region-level failure
• Small typos (wrong service name, wrong directory, missing DB) cost time, so runbooks matter
